# Release 132

Wild encounters use the existing active-play clock: Darkness weight is 0.05 in daylight and 3 at night; Light weight is 1 in daylight and 0 at night. Existing habitat and rarity weights are retained and normalized. Daylight includes morning/evening; night begins at 18:00 and ends at 06:00. Trainer teams and fixed legendary guardians retain their previous distribution. No save migration or asset changes.

Validation: 16 automated day/night encounter and elemental balance tests passed. All biome pools remain nonempty and normalized; clock boundaries, light exclusion, dark odds ratios, trainer bypass, guardians and starter-area level five verified. No phone/browser visual QA performed.
